--- title: Calendar description: Every UK food on one chart, week by week. permalink: /calendar/ charts: true --- {%- comment -%} One chart, baked by start. The order switch, the first row of the controls, is a radio group that assets/charts.js answers by reordering the chart's rows in place (By family, A to Z); it needs JavaScript, so it is shown only with it, and a no-JS visitor gets the chart by start. site.js draws the today line, keeps the sticky month header level with the chart and opens it at today; charts.js adds the time travel, the zoom and the food popover, and the list of foods for the chosen time under the chart. Every visit opens by start: the choice is not remembered. {%- endcomment -%}
