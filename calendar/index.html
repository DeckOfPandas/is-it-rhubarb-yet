--- title: Calendar description: Every UK food on one chart, week by week. permalink: /calendar/ charts: true --- {%- comment -%} Three copies of the chart, one per ordering, built at build time. The order switch, the first row of the controls, is a radio group; CSS shows the chart whose radio is checked, so it works without JavaScript. site.js draws the today line, keeps the sticky month header level with the chart and opens it at today; assets/charts.js adds the time travel, the zoom and the food popover, and the list of foods for the chosen time under the chart. Every visit opens by start: the choice is not remembered. {%- endcomment -%}
