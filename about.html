Is it rhubarb yet? Well, is it???

I can cook. I prefer to cook and eat food that:

  1. tastes its best,
  2. comes from the UK, and
  3. was produced sustainably.

I want a simple way to check what's in season, what's coming up, and what I should get a wiggle on and buy more of before it disappears again. This will help me plan fun and tasty meals, and not waste time and money on a January tomato.

This site answers the important rhubarb question, and the same for {{ others }} other things that grow, swim, run, or fly about in the UK.

The weekly and full-year views sort foods by category and season start, or alphabetically if for some reason you browse a supermarket for squid then immediately for strawberries.

The swanky infographics are here because, well, because I can #MoreIsMore.

Everything here is UK-produced, or sassy about why it isn't. Corrections welcome. Ish.

The site doesn't explain what dinner to make with a kohlrabi, or which drink to float edible flowers in, because I already know, or can find out using my handy triaging websites for food and cocktails.
