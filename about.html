Is it rhubarb yet?

I can cook. I prefer to cook and eat food when it tastes its best. I prefer to cook food grown sustainably, and in the UK.

I want a simple way to check what's in season, what's coming up, and what I should get a wiggle on and buy more of, so I can have interesting and tasty food in mind when I'm shopping.

This site answers the important rhubarb question, and the same for {{ others }} other things that grow, swim, run, or fly about in the UK. Each food gets a verdict, and a soothing quotation to help me handle the news.

The weekly and full-year views sort foods by category and season start, or alphabetically if for some reason you browse a supermarket for squid then immediately for strawberries.

The swanky infographics are here because, well, because I can.

Everything here is British or sassy. Corrections welcome. Ish.

This site doesn’t explain what dinner to make with a kohlrabi, or which drink to float edible flowers in, because I already know, or can find out using my handy food and cocktails recipe collections.
