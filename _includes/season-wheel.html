{% comment %} The season wheel for one food: the year as a ring, January at twelve o'clock, clockwise, one ring per variety, the centre cut out. The rings run from the longest season outside to the shortest inside, as the wheel's do (further out is longer): the baked `rings` order, from ringOrder in verdict.js. Each ring's data-ring is its variety's place in the data, which the ring list and the cards' dials look it up by. Expects: food. No trigonometry: every arc is a dashed circle. pathLength puts the dash units in weeks (52) or days (364), so a range from week a to b is a dash of length b-a+1 starting at offset a-1. Rotating the whole group by -90 degrees puts week 1 at the top. A fully in-season range is one dash in the ring's colour. A "just about" range is drawn a day at a time, each day mixed between the off-season grey and the colour by --t: rising into a season that follows it, falling out of one that precedes it, flat when it touches neither or both. A range sold from store is one dash, the colour faded well into the grey. A variety (or food) with `shades` sweeps those colours across its in-season run instead of a flat colour, and fades in from the first shade and out from the last. Each day (or shaded week) dash is two units long so it runs under the next one: a dash end that meets the grey through antialiasing shows as a hairline seam, so only the last dash of a run is allowed to end. With standalone=true the SVG is a file of its own, for /wheel/.svg: no figure or legend, an xmlns, its own styles in fixed colours, and a script that points the hand at today when the file is opened directly. The cards' dials are cut from that file (miniDial in site.js), so it is parsed as XML: every name is escaped and no attribute is bare. {% endcomment %} {%- assign food = include.food -%} {%- assign C = 180 -%}{%- assign R = 140 -%}{%- assign R0 = 78 -%} {%- assign n = food.varieties.size -%} {%- assign band = R | minus: R0 | times: 1.0 | divided_by: n -%} {%- assign thick = band | minus: 3 | round: 2 -%} {%- assign month_starts = "1,5,9,14,18,23,27,31,36,40,44,49" | split: "," -%} {%- assign month_names = "January,February,March,April,May,June,July,August,September,October,November,December" | split: "," -%} {%- unless include.standalone -%}{%- endunless -%}
