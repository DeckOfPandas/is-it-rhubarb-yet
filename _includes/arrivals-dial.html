{% comment %} A whole-table wheel for one kind of food (veg, fruit, wild, herbs, fish, meat). Expects: kind, id. Every food sits at the angle where its season begins and at a distance from the centre set by how long that season lasts: the shortest seasons hug the month ring, the longest reach the rim. Faint guide rings at one, three, six and nine months carry their own labels, so the chart needs no key. Read clockwise from the hand for what comes next. Foods in season all year have no beginning and are listed in the hole. A season "begins" at the first in-season week not joined to an in-season week before it (the year end counts as adjacent), so a season that wraps December into January begins in the autumn, not on 1 January. Its length is the longest variety's in-season weeks. Layout: foods are spread evenly across their month's arc (the data is month-grained, so the angle within a month carries no information). They are dealt into three angular passes by rank of length, so that labels next to each other differ in radius; foods of the same length that still land side by side are nudged 12 units outward, about two weeks. Entries are "m |slug|length" strings, so sorting as text orders by month, then length, then name. {% endcomment %} {%- assign C = 440 -%}{%- assign RING = 100 -%}{%- assign THICK = 16 -%}{%- assign R0
