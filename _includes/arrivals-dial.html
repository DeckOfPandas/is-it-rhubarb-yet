{% comment %} A whole-table wheel for one kind of food (veg, fruit, wild, herbs, fish, meat), or for every food (all). Expects: kind, id. Every food sits at the angle where its season begins and at a distance from the centre set by how long that season lasts: the shortest seasons hug the month ring, the longest reach the rim. Faint guide rings at one, three, six and nine months carry their own labels, and a light arrow runs out from the month ring past the last ring to the words "season length", so the chart needs no key. Read clockwise from the hand for what comes next. Foods in season all year have no beginning and are listed under the wheel, "All year:", each a link in its own colour. A season "begins" at the first in-season week not joined to an in-season week before it (the year end counts as adjacent), so a season that wraps December into January begins in the autumn, not on 1 January. Its length is the longest variety's in-season weeks. From each dot a thin arc in the food's colour sweeps clockwise for the length of the season, so the arc ends where the season ends and the dial doubles as a round calendar. Layout: foods are spread evenly across their month's arc (the data is month-grained, so the angle within a month carries no information). They are dealt into three angular passes by rank of length, so that labels next to each other differ in radius. Foods of the same length share a radius and so would share an arc; each is jittered a few units in or out by its rank among those equals, trading a little radial accuracy for arcs that can be told apart. Entries are "m |slug|length" strings, so sorting as text orders by month, then length, then name. {% endcomment %} {%- comment -%} Optional tuning, kept for trying variations: ring (month ring radius,
