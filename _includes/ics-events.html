{%- comment -%} The calendar events for one food: one all-day event, repeating yearly, on the first day of each fully-in-season run of each ring that gives the verdict (not another coast, a glasshouse or a wild ring beside a market one); none for a food that cannot be sold. A run that carries on from week 52 into week 1 is one season, which starts in its autumn run, not again on 1 January. Each event's UID is the food, the ring and which of the ring's seasons it is ("rhubarb-forced-1@is-it-rhubarb-yet"), not its date, so a calendar updates the event when the date moves rather than adding another. Expects: food, jan1 (1 January of the build year, in seconds).
