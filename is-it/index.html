--- title: Is it … yet? description: Ask whether a UK food is in season yet. permalink: /is-it/ --- {%- comment -%} One list of every food, in the shape of the weekly page's: each item carries its rank under two orders as --oa (A to Z) and --og (by family, A to Z within it), and the order switch picks which rank the grid lays out by. --os (newest first) needs today, so site.js sets it once the season data has loaded; until then that order is A to Z. Family headings are items that only show in family order. The state switch narrows the list to this week's going out, perfect now or coming in, again from the season data; without it the switch does nothing and the whole list shows. The header's search box, or ?q=, narrows it by words at the same time. A family heading with nothing left under it is hidden too. {%- endcomment -%}
