--- title: description: Is it in season yet? Type a UK food and get a yes, a no, or a when. --- {%- comment -%} The hook. A huge "Is it [ ] yet?" with the input inline. As the visitor types, the this-week blocks and the Everything list give way to the matches, directly under the hero, each with its verdict at the end of its row; clearing the input brings them back. Without JavaScript the whole list renders and the input is a form that reloads this page. Verdicts in the list are baked for the build day; site.js recomputes them when the visitor's week differs. The list is drawn twice, by family (the default) and A to Z, and the order switch shows one by CSS, as the calendar's does: lists in columns read down, then across, so one list cannot be reordered in place. The A to Z list carries the never-foods, hidden, and every row's search terms (name, aliases and shared words; site.js adds the varieties from foods.json), the names it completes to (its name and aliases) and its line, which the matches are copied from. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign baked = site.data.baked -%} {%- assign foods = site.data.foods | sort: "name" -%}
{%- comment -%} The hero is a form, not a heading, so the page's one heading is spoken but not seen. {%- endcomment -%}
