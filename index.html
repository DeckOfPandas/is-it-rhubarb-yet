--- title: description: Is it in season yet? Type a UK food and get a yes, a no, or a date. --- {%- comment -%} The hook. A huge "Is it [ ] yet?" with the input inline; as the visitor types, the list below narrows, each food's verdict word in its own colour at the end of its row. Enter, or a single remaining match, goes to the food page; no match gets the not-found line. Without JavaScript the whole list renders and the input is a form that reloads this page. Verdicts in the list are baked for the build day; site.js recomputes them when the visitor's week differs. The list is drawn twice, by family (the default) and A to Z, and the order switch shows one by CSS, as the calendar's does: lists in columns read down, then across, so one list cannot be reordered in place. The A to Z list carries the never-foods, hidden, and every row's search terms; typing hides both lists and shows the matches, best first, in a list of their own. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign baked = site.data.baked -%} {%- assign foods = site.data.foods | sort: "name" -%}
