--- title: description: Is it in season yet? Type a UK food and get a yes, a no, or a date. --- {%- comment -%} The hook. A huge "Is it [ ] yet?" with the input inline; as the visitor types, the list below narrows, each food showing its verdict word in its own colour. Enter, or a single remaining match, goes to the food page; no match gets the not-found line. Without JavaScript the whole list renders and the input is a form that reloads this page. Verdicts in the list are baked for the build day; site.js recomputes them when the visitor's week differs. The list is one grid whose items carry a rank under two orders as --oa (A to Z) and --og (by family, A to Z within it); the order switch picks which rank lays it out by, and family headings are items that only show in family order. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign baked = site.data.baked -%} {%- assign foods = site.data.foods | sort: "name" -%}
