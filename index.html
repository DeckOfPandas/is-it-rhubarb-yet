Rows are varieties, grouped by food group and sorted by the first week they are in season so the year reads as a wave. JavaScript adds only the "today" marker. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign x0 = 190 -%} {%- assign cw = 14 -%} {%- assign rh = 18 -%} {%- assign header = 44 -%} {%- assign group_gap = 26 -%} {%- assign month_starts = "1,5,9,14,18,23,27,31,36,40,44,49" | split: "," -%} {%- assign month_names = "Jan,Feb,Mar,Apr,May,Jun,Jul,Aug,Sep,Oct,Nov,Dec" | split: "," -%} {%- assign groups = "veg:Vegetables,salad:Salad,fruit:Fruit,nuts:Nuts,wild:Wild & foraged" | split: "," -%} {%- comment -%} Count rows first so the SVG can declare its height. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign rows = 0 -%} {%- for food in site.data.foods -%}{%- assign rows = rows | plus: food.varieties.size -%}{%- endfor -%} {%- assign height = rows | times: rh | plus: header | plus: 10 -%} {%- assign height = groups.size | times: group_gap | plus: height -%} {%- assign width = cw | times: 52 | plus: x0 | plus: 8 -%}

What's in season

Rows are foods, columns are weeks of the year. A full cell means in season; a pale cell means just coming in or going out.
