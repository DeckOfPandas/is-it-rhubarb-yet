The states are forced, not real: the script at the foot copies every :hover and :focus-visible rule in site.css onto the .is-hover and .is-focus columns, so a forced state is always the stylesheet's own and nothing here is styled twice. The components are the real ones, from their includes and classes, with foods picked from the baked verdicts. The search input needs the live hero, so its states are home itself in frames, by ?q=.
