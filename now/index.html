--- title: This week description: What is in season in the UK right now. permalink: /now/ --- {%- comment -%} All 52 weekly lists are rendered at build time. JavaScript reveals the one for today (or ?week=N) and wires the previous/next buttons. Without JavaScript every week shows, in order. For each food and week: "in" if any variety is fully in season; otherwise "out" (going out) if a variety's just-about range follows its in-season range, or "coming" if it precedes one. Year ends count as adjacent. {%- endcomment -%}
