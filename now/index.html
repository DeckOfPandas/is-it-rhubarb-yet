Each is split by kind of food and, within a kind, runs by time left. The build-day week is baked here from _data/baked.json; site.js redraws the lists from foods.json whenever the page was not baked today, and for the previous/next buttons, building the same markup as band.html. Without JavaScript the baked week shows, dated. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign baked = site.data.baked -%} {%- assign now = baked.now -%}
