--- title: Source data description: Where the seasons on this site come from, and how to get the data. permalink: /sources/ ---

Source data

UK seasons from the New Covent Garden Market calendar, cross-checked against BBC Good Food, with the shooting seasons from BASC and the fish from Flying Fish Seafoods' Cornish chart. Wild and foraged foods, the herbs, the meats and a few shellfish are still common knowledge: each of those pages says so until it has been checked against a named source.

Dates say "about" because the data is weekly and the weather is not. A season starting in a week means the first week it is normally worth asking for, in England, broadly; Scotland runs later.

The data

All of it as JSON: one object per food with its name, slug, group, colour, source and a list of varieties, each with its weeks as [from, to, state] runs on a 52-week year where week 1 starts on 1 January and state is in or avail. It and the calendar feed are CC BY 4.0; say where it came from.

Calendar feed

Subscribe to the season starts in your calendar app and every food's season arrives in it as an all-day event on the week it starts, "In season: Rhubarb (forced)", every year. Add it by its address, rather than by downloading the file, and it stays up to date by itself.

Something wrong? Open an issue with the food, the week and where you looked.
