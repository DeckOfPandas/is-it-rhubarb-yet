Dial candidates

The veg dial, which is the busiest, drawn six ways. Same data every time.

A. As it stands

Linear: five units a week. Arcs 2.5 wide at three-quarter strength. Dots 3.5.

B. Lighter touch

Thinner, paler arcs and bigger dots, so the dots lead and the arcs follow.

C. Log time

Radius grows with the logarithm of the season: a month, three months and a year are evenly spaced, so the crowd of short seasons gets the room and the year-long ones give some up. Lighter arcs as in B.

D. Roomier

The month ring shrinks to 70 so the one-month line sits closer to the centre and everything outside it gets more space. Lighter arcs as in B.

E. Log time, roomier

C and D together.

F. Dots only

No arcs: distance from the centre is the only measure of length. What the Dots setting on The year shows.
